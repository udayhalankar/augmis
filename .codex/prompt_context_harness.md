## Current Focus
- Repository, business-area, and intelligence-pattern metadata should be DB-backed rather than file-backed.
- Business-area intelligence is the canonical product language across settings, dashboards, search, document intelligence, and escalations.
- Search, document intelligence, executive summary, and escalation logic should rely on configured business-area rules and pattern metadata instead of fixed domain assumptions.
- OCR, indexing quality, and repository sync reliability remain important because downstream intelligence depends on indexed facts.
- Executive dashboard risk visuals should stay aligned with extracted-fact AI risk signals and expose exact matched records for drill-down.
- AI Copilot should switch to structured multi-business-area risk summarization for broad all-repository questions instead of overfitting to the top retrieved chunk set.
- Copilot routing and coverage instructions should remain dynamic and derived from runtime business-area evidence, without hardcoded named business areas.

## Parked Items
- Legacy specialized intelligence surfaces may still exist internally while the app transitions to fully business-area-driven intelligence.
- Additional structured extraction engines can be introduced later for domains that currently use the generic intelligence workflow.
